Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Kangaroo Math Prep contains all 1,000 competition questions and sound effects bundled directly inside the app. You can practice on airplanes, during road trips, or anywhere without Wi-Fi.

2. How does progress sync between iPhone and iPad?

If your devices are logged into the same Apple iCloud ID, your streaks, stars, bookmarks, and mistake notebook automatically sync in the background via Apple's encrypted Key-Value Store without requiring any login credentials or account creation.

3. How do I change grade divisions or scoring rules?

Tap the Gear icon on the home dashboard to enter Parent Settings. From here, you can switch between Grade 1–2 (Exploring), Grade 3–4 (Navigating), Grade 5–6 (Discovering), or Grade 7–8 (Investigating), and select either USA Rules (no penalty) or International Rules (-1 pt deduction for incorrect answers).

4. How do I report an issue or math discrepancy?

During any practice question, tap the question feedback flag icon. You can select the reason and enter brief notes. Parents can review, copy, or email the diagnostic summary directly from Parent Settings.

5. What is the Parent Gate?

To comply with Apple's strict Kids Category guidelines and protect children, access to settings, data resets, and external contact requires passing an adult verification challenge (either answering an adult arithmetic question or verifying an adult birth year).
