Privacy Policy

Kangaroo Math Prep (iOS & iPadOS) • COPPA & Kids Category Compliant

✓ 100% Offline & Zero Data Collection

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Kangaroo Math Prep ("App", "we", "us"). We are fully committed to protecting the privacy, security, and safety of children, parents, and educators. This policy satisfies Apple App Store Guidelines for the Kids Category (Guideline 1.3) and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

1. Zero Personal Data Collection Guarantee

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No Accounts or Sign-In: No registration, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords are ever requested.
  • No Tracking Identifiers: We do not collect IDFA, IDFV, advertising identifiers, or device hardware fingerprints.
  • No Location Access: The App never requests access to GPS, Wi-Fi location, or geographic coordinates.
  • No Media or Sensor Access: The App does not access your camera, microphone, photo library, or contacts.

2. 100% On-Device Local Storage

All student progress, test scores, streak counts, bookmarked questions, mistake notebook records, and user preferences are stored exclusively on your local device (via standard local sandbox storage). None of this data is ever uploaded to external servers or cloud services. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Tracking

In accordance with strict Kids Category guidelines:

  • No Ads: There are zero third-party advertisements or commercial ad network SDKs.
  • No Tracking: There are zero third-party analytics trackers (no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, etc.).
  • No In-App Purchases: The App is completely free with no subscriptions or payment processing.

4. Parental Gate Protection

All administrative settings, data resets, and external documentation references are protected behind an interactive Parental Verification Gate requiring multi-step arithmetic problem verification before access is granted.

5. Compliance with COPPA & International Standards

Because the App collects zero personal information from any user, including children under 13, it fully complies with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the European GDPR / GDPR-K, and California consumer privacy statutes.
