📡 FIELD TERMINAL 09 • W6NWB ATTIC LINK

No Way Back

The Last Frequency · An Interactive Mars Survival Thriller
Emergency Carrier · 437.525 MHz

Happening right now. From your attic on Earth, you listen to an old emergency radio channel nobody monitors anymore.

One night, a voice cuts through the static: Mara Dawson, a young systems engineer on Mars, is trapped in a collapsed service bay at Morrow Station. The main radio is dead, her tablet battery is dropping, and a dust front is closing the escape route.

The planetary tracking feeds on Earth show all-clear. The raw telemetry tells a different story. You are 140 million miles away with a microphone, an amateur dish, and a field logbook. Can you help guide her home?

⏱️

Speed-of-Light Realism

Radio signals take ~11 minutes to cross between Earth and Mars. Every message travels into the void, forcing you to plan ahead and live with the anticipation.
