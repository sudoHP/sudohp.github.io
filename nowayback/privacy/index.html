Privacy Policy

No Way Back: Beyond (iOS & iPadOS)

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how No Way Back: Beyond ("the Application", "we", "us", developed by sudoHP) handles your information. Our core philosophy is simple: your gameplay and personal data belong exclusively to you on your device. We do not collect, track, share, or monetize any of your data.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection Guarantee

We do not collect, log, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • No Account Creation: You do not need to register, provide an email address, create a password, or sign in to any third-party service.
  • No Identity or Device Tracking: We do not access, store, or transmit your Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA), Vendor Identifier (IDFV), device IMEI, MAC address, or IP address.
  • No Location Access: The Application never requests access to your GPS, Wi-Fi location, or geographic telemetry.
  • No Device Sensors or Media: The Application does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or biometric hardware.

2. 100% On-Device Local Sandbox Storage

All game progress—including story state, unlocked transmissions, logbook entries, player callsigns, audio volume settings, and ending achievements—is saved strictly within your device's isolated local app container (Apple iOS Sandboxing).

None of this information is ever uploaded to external servers, cloud databases, or developer servers. If you choose to delete the Application from your device, all local save data is permanently erased.

3. Zero Third-Party Trackers & Zero Ads

The Application contains:

  • No Advertising SDKs: Zero commercial advertisements, banners, interstitial videos, or sponsored links.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: No tracking SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, Adjust, or AppsFlyer).
  • No In-App Purchases or Financial Data: No payment tokens, card numbers, or transaction logs are collected or handled by us.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because No Way Back collects zero personal information from any user of any age, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
