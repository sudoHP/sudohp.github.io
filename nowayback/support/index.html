Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the core gameplay premise of No Way Back?

You play as an amateur radio operator in an attic listening to a retired emergency channel (437.525 MHz). You establish two-way communication with Mara Dawson, an engineer trapped at Morrow Station on Mars following an orbital disaster. Through text transmissions, tactical decision prompts, telemetry analysis, and map cross-referencing, you must guide her across the Martian surface before power expires.

2. How does the radio signal delay work?

In accordance with the physical laws of orbital mechanics, light-speed signals between Earth and Mars take approximately 11 minutes each way. When you transmit an outgoing message, a transit indicator displays the packet travel time. By default, you can tap [Continue] to instantly fast-forward to packet arrival, or experience the real-time anticipation based on your gameplay preferences.

3. Can I adjust message pacing and sound effects?

Yes. Tap the Gear icon at any time to open Settings. From here, you can toggle audio effects (receiver static, incoming carrier beeps, Morse tones), adjust text typewriter speeds, and control story pacing transitions.

4. Does the app require Wi-Fi or cellular data?

No. No Way Back is 100% offline. All 9 story chapters, branch trees, audio waveforms, character portraits, telemetry displays, and save states are bundled directly into the app bundle. You can play during flights, commutes, or in areas with no connectivity.

5. How does game saving work?

The game saves your progress automatically after every transmission and choice branch into your device's secure local sandbox. If you close the app or switch tasks, you can resume exactly where you left off. You can also inspect completed transmission history and unlocked records inside the Field Logbook.

6. Are there multiple endings?

Yes. Your tactical decisions—such as route navigation (ridge lines vs. shadowed valleys), resource management (rover battery allocation, life support preservation), and interpersonal trust—lead to three distinctly earned narrative endings.

7. How do I report an issue or typo during TestFlight beta testing?

If you encounter a visual glitch, layout issue, or typo while running a TestFlight build, you can take a screenshot on your iOS device and choose Share Beta Feedback. Alternatively, you can email diagnostic details, device model, and iOS version directly to sudohp.dev@gmail.com.
